# NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT LAWS

Preserve or strengthen these laws unless a formally approved constitutional change replaces them:

1. The user owns data, keys, policies, exports, and model choices.
2. Original evidence remains distinct from derived knowledge.
3. Every material derived claim can point to supporting evidence.
4. Human-authored content remains readable and exportable outside the product.
5. AI cannot silently alter canonical truth.
6. Messages do not automatically become canonical knowledge.
7. Private Vault context never enters a shared Space without explicit authority.
8. Agent identity remains distinguishable from human identity.
9. Every agent has an accountable human or organizational sponsor.
10. Read access, context access, action authority, consent, purpose, and delegation are distinct.
11. Every consequential action records actor, principal, authority, evidence, result, and recovery path.
12. Agents receive least privilege and short-lived, run-specific authority where possible.
13. Extensions never receive direct canonical-database access.
14. External protocols remain adapters rather than the internal domain model.
15. No UI-only capability is considered complete.
16. Every first-party capability must have governed commands, queries, events, permissions, provenance, export, and tests.
17. Search, vector, graph, cache, and other indexes remain derived and rebuildable.
18. No AI provider, database, agent framework, sync engine, cloud, or workflow engine is irreplaceable.
19. Local-first operation and credible self-hosting remain strategic capabilities.
20. Cloud use is explicit and policy-controlled.
21. Automation begins in shadow mode and earns bounded autonomy through evidence.
22. Uncertain identity, authority, provenance, or state fails closed.
23. The moat must not depend on hostage data or blocked export.
24. Domain-specific semantics enter through Domain Packs, profiles, schemas, workflows, policies, and views before the universal kernel changes.
25. Core changes must be required by at least two materially different domains and must be expressible without domain-specific terminology.
26. Different objects may require different consistency, collaboration, retention, and safety models.
27. Observed, reported, derived, assumed, simulated, predicted, intended, commanded, and confirmed states remain distinguishable.
28. Physical, financial, legal, medical, destructive, or public actions receive stricter authority than ordinary internal drafting.
29. Every design must include migration, rollback, export, recovery, and operational ownership.
30. MindVault remains separate from DevX Studio, Meridian, CodePark, and DevX Runtime. Integrate through explicit contracts rather than conflating product ownership.
